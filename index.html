How to Play CineSpotle

1. Guess in 10 Tries: Use the Format and Industry dropdowns to filter, then search any movie, TV series, or anime powered by TMDB and Google AI Search.

2. Read the Attribute Card: Every guess reveals a full comparison card with Year, Box Office, Primary Studio, Rated, side-by-side IMDb & Letterboxd, Director, Producer, Lead Actor, and Supporting Cast. Scroll down anytime to inspect previous guesses.

3. Neon Colour & Role Clues: Neon Emerald means an exact match. Neon Amber means close (within 3 years, 0.5 IMDb, 0.3 Letterboxd, 30% box office) — or a cross-role cast match (your guessed Lead is in the answer's Supporting Cast, or vice versa). Arrows (↑ / ↓) show higher or lower.

4. Choose 3 of 5 Hints After 5 Tries: After 5 guesses, Hints (0/3) unlocks with 5 selectable categories: Tagline/Quote, Lead Actor's Other Works, Director's Other Works, 3–5 Word Essence, and Production Company & Distributor.
