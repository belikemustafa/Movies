How to Play
CineSpotle Guessing Rules
1. Set or Pick a Mystery Target

Search any movie, TV series, or anime in the search box to lock it as the mystery target, or click Random Pick (R) for a live TMDB title.

2. Read the Color Clues

Green means an exact match (within ±0.1 on separate IMDb & Letterboxd tiles, or ±$12M on Box Office). Yellow with ↑ / ↓ means you are close.

3. Hints & Matched Soundtrack

Use Google Search AI clues anytime, unlock subtle clues on Guess 3, and play a 12-second clip of the real matched soundtrack on Guess 5.
